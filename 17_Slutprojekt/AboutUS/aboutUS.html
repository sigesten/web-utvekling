About Us

At Auerum, we believe that art is more than decoration—it is expression, identity, and connection. Based in Malmö, Sweden, we are a contemporary art company dedicated to offering high-quality pieces that inspire and elevate spaces around the world.

Our carefully curated collection brings together emerging and established artists, blending modern creativity with timeless craftsmanship. Each piece we offer is selected with a focus on originality, quality, and emotional impact, ensuring that every artwork tells a story worth sharing.

While our roots are in Malmö, our vision is global. Auerum connects art lovers, collectors, and creators across borders, making exceptional art accessible wherever you are. Whether you're looking to transform your home, workspace, or personal collection, we aim to provide artwork that resonates on a deeper level.

We are passionate about supporting artists and fostering creativity, building a platform where art can thrive and be appreciated in all its forms.
