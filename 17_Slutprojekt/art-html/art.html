Our latest Art Instalments

Twisted delight

A hypnotic spiral of black and gold pulls the eye inward, evoking a sense of motion frozen in time. The swirling contrasts of dark and warm tones create a tension between chaos and harmony — a piece that invites the viewer to lose themselves in its depth.

Sun of the tree

A solitary tree stands bold against a blazing sky of amber and cobalt, its silhouette a symbol of resilience and quiet strength. The dramatic interplay of warm and cool tones captures the fleeting beauty of dusk — a timeless moment suspended between day and night.

Painted sea

Fierce strokes of crimson, teal, and gold collide like waves crashing against an unseen shore. This piece channels the raw, untamed energy of the ocean — wild, unpredictable, and endlessly alive. A celebration of colour in its most expressive form.

Waivy Colors

Fluid ribbons of yellow, black, and teal weave together in a dance of organic rhythm. The undulating forms suggest both movement and calm, as if colour itself has found a natural current to follow. A piece that breathes with every glance.

Dotted splash

Bold splashes of orange and black burst across the canvas, punctuated by playful circular accents. The raw energy of this piece feels spontaneous and alive — as though captured mid-motion, freezing a single explosive moment of creative force.

Starligt favorit

Warm golden light radiates outward through layers of burnt orange and deep ochre, evoking the quiet glow of a setting sun on the horizon. This piece carries a sense of warmth and nostalgia — a familiar light seen through an entirely new lens.

Dark souls

Deep navy and midnight black swirl together in a turbulent vortex of emotion and shadow. This haunting composition speaks to the complexity of the human spirit — the weight we carry, the depths we explore, and the beauty that can emerge from darkness.

Happy cat

A joyful explosion of pink, blue, and yellow shapes dance freely across the canvas, radiating playfulness and light. This vibrant piece captures the pure, uncomplicated happiness of a carefree moment — colour as an expression of delight in its simplest form.
